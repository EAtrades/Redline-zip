Questions

What does the red line mean?+

It marks a liquidity grab on fast volume: a spot where the move may be exhausted and price could top or bottom out. It's a level to watch for a reaction, not a guaranteed reversal.

Is this a signal service?+

No. Redline marks levels and plots setups; you make the call. It makes your read faster and more consistent.
