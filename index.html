Questions

What does the red line mean?+

It marks a liquidity grab on fast volume: a spot where the move may be exhausted and price could top or bottom out. It's a level to watch for a reaction, not a guaranteed reversal.

How often do trade ideas show up?+

Not often, on purpose. A trade idea only fires when every required box on the checklist passes, including exhaustion. Most redlines won't qualify. When one doesn't, the dashboard tells you exactly what's missing.

Is this a signal service?+

No. Redline marks levels and, when its checklist passes, plots a trade idea, but you make the call and manage the trade. It makes your read faster and more consistent.

Which markets and timeframes?+

Built for NQ and ES futures, including the micros. Executed on the 1–5m with 15m, 30m and 4H context.

Do I need to know ICT / SMC?+

It helps. If you're newer, join the free Discord and watch a few live sessions to see how each level is used.
